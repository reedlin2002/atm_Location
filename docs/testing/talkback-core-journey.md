# TalkBack core journey

Run on a real Android phone in light and dark system themes, once at default
font size and once at 200%.

1. Enable TalkBack and launch a fresh install.
2. Complete or skip onboarding without granting location.
3. Open manual search, enter an offline query, and choose an ATM result.
4. Ignore the map. Move through the ATM list; confirm each row announces bank,
   place, address, distance, selection state, and a separate favorite action.
5. Select a row, open its summary, then open details.
6. Confirm capabilities say confirmed, unsupported, or unknown rather than
   communicating only through color.
7. Activate navigation and verify the chooser receives the ATM destination.
8. Verify share, report, filters, filter removal, settings, reset confirmation,
   errors, and loading states have concise labels and predictable focus order.
9. Repeat in landscape and on a tablet; verify primary actions remain
   reachable and no text overlaps or clips.

Record device model, Android version, app build, theme, font scale, TalkBack
version, date, tester, outcome, and screenshots or video for failures.
