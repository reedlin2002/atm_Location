# Closed test and first submission runbook

This is owner-operated. It requires a verified Play developer account,
payments, identity/device checks, signing secrets, legal declarations and real
testers. Support contact: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

1. Create the Play app with the release application ID, enable Play App
   Signing, register the upload certificate, and add a public HTTPS privacy
   policy URL.
2. Upload Issue 32's immutable candidate to internal testing. Match the AAB
   SHA-256, version, mapping and source revision to the candidate manifest.
3. Resolve every actionable pre-launch report finding or record why it is not
   applicable. Repeat on at least one low-memory Android 10 device and one
   current Android device.
4. Run the full owner checklist: first launch; location deny/allow/permanent
   deny; airplane-mode search; list and map; dense clusters; details; filters;
   favorites; navigation; share; email report; reset; 200% text; TalkBack;
   dark theme; diagnostics opt-in, opt-out and console visibility.
5. Complete Data safety, content rating, target audience, ads, App access and
   Financial features from the actual candidate and retain dated screenshots.
6. Upload the zh-TW listing, icon, feature graphic, phone/tablet screenshots,
   contact details, sources, license, update frequency and disclaimers.
7. If the account is subject to the personal-account closed-test rule shown in
   Play Console, keep at least 12 owner-approved testers opted in continuously
   for 14 days. Record join/leave dates, device coverage, feedback, fixes and
   any clock reset. Do not recruit testers without owner approval.
8. Request production access when eligible. Submit Taiwan as the initial
   country and do not assume the first release supports staged rollout.
9. After approval, install from the public listing and verify launch, restricted
   Maps key, catalog update, privacy URL, screenshots and support email.
10. For later versions define staged-rollout stop/expand thresholds using
    consented crash diagnostics and support reports, never behavioral tracking.

Requirements can change. The owner must use the exact Play Console prompts
shown on submission day instead of treating this runbook as a legal or policy
guarantee.
